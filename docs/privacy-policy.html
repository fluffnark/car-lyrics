Car Lyrics Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 20, 2026

Car Lyrics is a private Android Auto prototype developed by Caleb Cheng ("we", "us", or "our"). This policy explains what the app does with information when you use it.

Information we collect

Car Lyrics does not require an account and does not operate a server. We do not collect, sell, or share your name, email address, contacts, precise location, payment information, or usage analytics.

The app stores your queue, saved videos, local playlists, and related song metadata on your Android device so those features work between sessions. This local information is not uploaded to us.

When Android Auto provides vehicle speed to the app, Car Lyrics uses it only in memory for the car display. It does not store or transmit vehicle speed, location, or driving history.

Network and third-party services

Car Lyrics connects to the internet to load public karaoke video listings and to play selected videos through YouTube's embedded player. YouTube and Google may process requests, cookies, device information, and viewing activity under their own policies. YouTube may also show advertising or restrict playback.

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • YouTube Terms of Service

Car Lyrics does not receive or store your YouTube password, Google password, or YouTube account credentials.

Data security and retention

Local queue, playlist, and saved-video data remains on the device until you remove it, clear the app's storage, or uninstall the app. Because Car Lyrics does not maintain an account or backend, we cannot recover that local data.

Children's privacy

Car Lyrics is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app's behavior changes. The effective date above identifies the latest version.

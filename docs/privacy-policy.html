Car Lyrics Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 22, 2026

Car Lyrics is a private Android Auto prototype developed by Caleb Cheng ("we", "us", or "our"). This policy explains what the app does with information when you use it.

Information we collect

Car Lyrics does not require an account and does not operate a server. We do not collect, sell, or share your name, email address, contacts, precise location, payment information, or usage analytics.

The app stores your queue, saved videos, local playlists, local playback history with resume positions, and related song metadata on your Android device so those features work between sessions. This local information is not uploaded to us.

Car Lyrics does not read or store vehicle speed, location, or driving history.

Network and third-party services

Car Lyrics connects to the internet to load public karaoke video listings and to open selected videos in the installed Morphe YouTube app (or through the older embedded player). YouTube and Google may process requests, cookies, device information, and viewing activity under their own policies. YouTube may also show advertising or restrict playback.

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • YouTube Terms of Service

Car Lyrics does not receive or store your YouTube password, Google password, or YouTube account credentials.

Optional Spotify playlist import

When you connect Spotify, Car Lyrics requests read-only access to your private and collaborative playlists. Browser sign-in uses PKCE; the app never receives your Spotify password or a client secret. Access and refresh tokens are encrypted with a key held in Android Keystore. Imported playlist names, track titles, artists, matching results, and selected karaoke video IDs stay in the app’s private storage. CSV imports read only the file you select.

To find karaoke versions, the app sends song titles and artist names to YouTube search. Spotify access is governed by Spotify’s Privacy Policy. Car Lyrics does not change Spotify playlists or download Spotify audio. Disconnect removes connection tokens; imported karaoke playlists remain until app storage is cleared or the app is uninstalled. Access can also be revoked in Spotify account settings.

Voice and text search

Voice recognition is provided by Android Auto or the speech service installed on your phone. That provider may process audio under its own privacy policy. Car Lyrics receives the recognized words, not the microphone recording, and sends your search text to YouTube to retrieve public video results. Car Lyrics does not record microphone audio, operate a speech server, or upload searches to us.

Screen sharing and playback controls

With your Android screen-sharing approval, Car Lyrics captures the selected app’s video frames in memory and displays them through Android Auto. It does not record or upload those frames to a server. Android notification access is used only to identify and control Morphe’s media session and read playback state; Car Lyrics does not read or save notification contents. Google sign-in and viewing activity remain in the selected player.

Local playback history and Morphe Home

Car Lyrics keeps up to 200 recently opened or identifiable Morphe videos with titles and available playback positions in private device storage. With playback-control access enabled, it can record identifiable Morphe playback while the phone UI is closed. Remove entries or clear local history from History options. It does not fetch or synchronize YouTube account watch history. Home reads recommendation titles and channel names from Morphe on the helper-owned display and renders native lists inside Car Lyrics. It sends those titles/channel names to public YouTube search and metadata endpoints to verify video links and fetches public thumbnails. Recommendation cards remain in memory. Sign-in and personalized recommendation processing remain in Morphe and YouTube; Car Lyrics does not read cookies or account credentials.

Optional native Morphe mode

With optional Shizuku authorization, Car Lyrics starts a local helper to open the installed Morphe app on a separate display and show its frames in Android Auto. This mode does not capture the phone screen. The helper only exposes Morphe display, Home, video-launch and recommendation-card reading operations to Car Lyrics; it does not read your accounts, messages, or files. You can revoke this access in Shizuku. Frames remain in memory and are not recorded or uploaded by Car Lyrics.

Data security and retention

Local queue, playlist, local history, and saved-video data remains on the device until you remove it, clear the app's storage, or uninstall the app. Because Car Lyrics does not maintain an account or backend, we cannot recover that local data.

Children's privacy

Car Lyrics is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app's behavior changes. The effective date above identifies the latest version.
